When __USER_FIRST__ says "work on <id>" (attended) or the worker picks a __ITEM__ up (unattended):

1. **Title first.** Set the session title to "__ITEM__ <id>" and save the prompt verbatim to the
   folder. They browse history by id; a clever title makes it unfindable.
2. **Is it a repeat?** Search `archive\` and [[lesson_ledger]] for the same requester AND subject.
   A "what's the status of..." ask means find the original reply before re-diagnosing. Scan the
   open queue for the same-week twin.
3. **Prior art.** History first, then the skill, the playbook, the procedure page. Search by key
   words, never the new id.
4. **Who owns the fix?** Run the family's first move from [[family_first_moves]] before any
   deeper diagnosis.
5. **Solve everything in reach**, stage the rest, never close on hand-keyed data.
6. **Draft once**, after the work is done, in their voice. Every other person the fix touches
   gets their own draft.
7. **Write back** summary.json and the report line; regenerate the dashboard once at the end.

**Why:** the corrections that repeat most are a reply written before the work was done, a
re-diagnosis of something already solved, and a deliverable that cannot be found later.

**How to apply:** follow the order; when a step is impossible, say which and why under
"Couldn't do", and keep going with the rest.
